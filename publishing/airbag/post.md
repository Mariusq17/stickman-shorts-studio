## YouTube Shorts title
Your Airbag Is a Tiny Explosion

## YouTube description
An airbag system built step by step: a folded nylon bag in the steering wheel, a gas generator, the crash sensor (accelerometer), the airbag computer that tells a pothole from a crash in milliseconds, the igniter and the nitrogen gas, the tear seam, inflation in about 30 milliseconds, vent holes for a soft landing, the powder 'smoke', the seatbelt pretensioner and the passenger seat weight sensor.
