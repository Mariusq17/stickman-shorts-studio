## YouTube Shorts title
How an ATM Knows Two Notes Are Stuck Together

## YouTube description
Inside a cash machine, built step by step: a pick roller peels off one note at a time, two rollers
measure every note's thickness to catch doubles, a flap sends suspicious notes to the reject bin,
light beams count and check length, a stacker builds one neat bundle, forgotten cash is retracted,
cards come back before cash, and the money sits in a steel safe with ink that stains stolen notes.
