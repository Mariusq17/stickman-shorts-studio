## YouTube Shorts title
Your Card Has No Battery. So How Does It Pay?

## YouTube description
Contactless payment explained, built step by step: the terminal's magnetic field powers the chip by induction, NFC keeps the range to a few centimetres, a one-time cryptogram makes copied data useless, and the payment travels through the shop's bank and the card network to your bank for fraud checks and approval, in about a second. Plus why phones send a token instead of your card number.
