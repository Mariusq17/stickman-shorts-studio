Why AI Chatbots Type One Word at a Time

## YouTube description
How large language models work, built step by step: a machine that guesses the next word, feeds its
own guess back in, trains on a mountain of text by tuning billions of parameters on graphics chips,
uses attention to know what "it" refers to, reads tokens instead of letters, rolls weighted dice
(temperature), learns manners from human feedback, and why it can be wrong with total confidence.
