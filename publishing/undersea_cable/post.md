## YouTube Shorts title
Why a Ship Goes Fishing for the Internet

## YouTube description
Almost all data between continents travels through cables on the ocean floor. We build one from a glass thread and a laser: optical fiber, repeaters that boost the light, electricity sent through the cable to power them, a garden-hose-thin cable in the deep sea, steel armor near the coast, many colors of light per fiber, landing stations, and repair ships when a cable breaks.
